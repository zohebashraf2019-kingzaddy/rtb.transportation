FAQ

Common questions

How is material priced?

Material is sold and delivered by the tonnage. Tell us the material, quantity and delivery address and we’ll give you a quote. Not sure how many tons you need? Send us the area dimensions and depth and we’ll help you figure it out.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Sun Valley and serve Reno, Sparks, Fernley and the surrounding Northern Nevada areas.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Yes, when the schedule allows. Call or text early in the day for the best chance at same-day service.

Can you haul dirt away instead of delivering it?

Absolutely. We do both import and export — bring clean fill onto your site or haul unwanted dirt, weeds, rock and debris off of it.

Do you work with contractors?

Yes. We haul for contractors, landscapers and businesses, including paving and construction production days.

What if the material I need isn’t listed?

If a quarry nearby carries it, we can deliver it. Just ask.
